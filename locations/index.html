Locations

SemRede 2026 runs from October 19 to 24 in Coimbra, Portugal. Five days at Eva Farm, then one open day in the city.

Monday to Friday / October 19 to 23

Eva Farm

Coimbra district

A working farm and learning space for a regenerative future. This is where the week happens: hands-on sessions, talks, meals and camping.

Getting there

  • The farm is outside the city, so plan the last stretch. Most people arrive by car or share a ride.
  • Coming by train or bus? Get to Coimbra first (below), then arrange a lift in the Rides & logistics part of the forum or in the chat.
  • The exact address and the pickup points are published closer to the event, together with the arrival times.

What to bring

  • Tent and sleeping gear if you camp, plus something warm: October nights in central Portugal get cold.
  • Boots or shoes you do not mind getting muddy, a torch, a water bottle.
  • Whatever you want to show or work on: radios, antennas, boards, tools, seeds.
Saturday / October 24

Edifício Embaixada

Coimbra, city centre

One day, open to all: talks, demos, a community fair, networking and the closing celebration, in the heart of Coimbra.

Getting there

  • In the city centre, so it is walkable from the old town and reachable by city bus.
  • From Coimbra-B station take the shuttle train to Coimbra-A, which puts you in the centre in about five minutes.
  • Driving in the centre is slow and parking is limited. Leave the car outside the centre and walk.
  • The street address and the door times are published closer to the event.
Travel

Getting to Coimbra

By train

Coimbra sits on the main Lisbon to Porto line. Long distance trains (Alfa Pendular and Intercidades) stop at Coimbra-B, north of the centre, and a short shuttle train runs from there to Coimbra-A in the middle of town, included in the ticket. Roughly two hours from Lisbon and about an hour from Porto. Book at cp.pt.

By bus

Coaches from Lisbon, Porto, Faro and across Europe stop at the Coimbra bus terminal near the centre. Rede Expressos and FlixBus both serve the city, and tickets are usually cheaper than the train.

By plane

The nearest airports are Porto (about 120 km) and Lisbon (about 200 km). Both have a train or metro link to the main station, and from there a direct train to Coimbra. Porto is usually the shorter trip.

By car

Coimbra is on the A1 motorway between Lisbon and Porto. If you have spare seats, offer them in the forum: someone is always looking for a ride.
