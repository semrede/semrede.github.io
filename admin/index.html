This page is for the SemRede admin key and the moderators it names. Log in with that key to use it; everyone else sees nothing to do here.

Every action here is a signed NOSTR event. There is no password and no server: what you publish is what the site reads back.

Moderation teamOnly the admin can change this

Moderators can hide accounts, pin threads and close threads. They cannot remove each other.

Hidden accountsHidden in the chat, the forum and the counters

Hiding someone adds them to your own mute list. Their posts stay on the relays and other NOSTR apps still show them; this site does not.

ThreadsPinned to the top, or closed to new replies

You can also pin and close from the thread itself; this is for when you have the link at hand.
