Offgrid messaging

XPRS

xprs.dev

The eXtended Packet Radio System: short text messages that travel over whatever is around, Bluetooth, LoRa, WiFi, amateur radio or the internet, and hop device to device when none of it is there. Messages are 250 bytes of plain text, the same format on every medium, encrypted when you want them private.

Your identity is a key made on your own device. Nobody issues it and nobody can revoke it, so there are no accounts and no servers to ask permission from. Devices take roles: station, relay, carrier, gateway, file server, archiver.

There are apps for Android, Linux and Windows, and firmware for LoRa boards like the T-Dongle-S3, M5Stack and Heltec. The code is BSD licensed, and it borrows from Reticulum, NOSTR and good old APRS.

Preparedness and community

GNPA

gnpa.pt

Preparação e Autossuficiência, "porque sobreviver não basta". An app that puts a community's preparedness in one place, in Portuguese, English, French, Spanish and German.

  • SOS map: ask for help, follow how an emergency is unfolding and coordinate with the people nearby.
  • Points of interest: a map the community fills in together, water, shelter, supplies, people who know things.
  • Academy: webinars, workshops and the literature worth reading.
  • Donations: a way to back projects and communities that need it.
