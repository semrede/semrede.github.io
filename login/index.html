How this works
  • No email, no phone number, no personal data. An account here is a pair of keys made in your browser. You choose a name, and that is all we ever see.
  • You can stay anonymous. Use any name you like, and nothing ties the account to you unless you write it yourself.
  • Your account is a NOSTR key. The same key works in the chat, the forum, registration and private messages, and in any other NOSTR app.
  • An extension keeps the key out of this site. An account made here is stored in this browser only, so back it up.
  • Posts in the chat and the forum are public. Messages are encrypted, but the relays still see who writes to whom.
