Tickets

100 free, numbered tickets. The event costs nothing, but there is food, water, space and beds for 100 people, so entry is by ticket. Ask for up to four, for you and whoever comes with you, and the numbered tickets arrive in your messages.

The Saturday grill is the one thing you pay for, and it is paid in CryptoEscudos you mine yourself. Bring about 5000 CESC.

Which parts are you coming to? Change your answer whenever you want

How many tickets?

You and up to three people coming with you. No names needed: all the tickets are held by your account, and you show them at the entrance. Pick the number before pressing "I'm going": tickets cannot be given back, only added.

Your answer and this comment are public NOSTR events, like a post. Anyone can read them and count them, which is how the numbers above work. For anything private, write to the organizers.

How the tickets work

"I'm going" to either part asks for your tickets: one for you and one for each person coming with you, up to four. Every ticket gets its own number, in the order people ask, and the organizers send them to you as a private message, usually within a day. After ticket 100 there is a waiting list, and the organizers let people in from it as they can.

Your tickets are tied to your NOSTR account on this site (the key this browser made for you, with no email and no password), not to names. At the entrance you open this page: every ticket has its own QR code, and the door scans one per person, for you and for whoever comes with you.

The account can go with you: on your account page, show and copy your key, then paste it at /login on your phone or any other device, and the same tickets are there. Keep a copy somewhere safe: a lost key is a lost ticket.

Questions?

Write to the organizers from your messages: that conversation is encrypted and only they can read it. For questions other people may also have, use the forum.
