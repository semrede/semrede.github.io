Where this comes fromAnd what it does not say

Every page view sends one small encrypted message. It carries the page, the referring domain, the browser language, a screen size, whether that browser had been here before and roughly how long the page was open. Nothing that identifies a person, no address, no cookie. People who ask not to be tracked are never counted, and /login, /messages and /admin never send anything.

A visit is one browser tab on one day, never "a person". Two tabs are two visits. Visits that end by closing the last tab are often missed, so the real numbers are higher than these.
