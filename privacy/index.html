12 / Privacy

Pictures & privacy

Photos and video are taken at SemRede and published here, in the chat and the forum, and by people posting their own afterwards. If you do not want to appear, here is how to say so.

Saying no

Wear the X

Wear a clip with a white X somewhere people can see it: on your shirt, your bag strap, your cap. It means do not photograph me, and do not publish anything I am in.

We hand out these clips at the entrance of both venues, so you can pick one up when you arrive. You can also print one at home and bring it.

  • Photographers at the event are told to look for the X before they shoot, and to skip anyone wearing one.
  • If you end up in a picture anyway, tell us and it goes away.
  • No clip at hand? Say it out loud, or draw an X on a piece of tape. It counts the same.
What we do

What gets published

Talks and workshops are filmed or photographed, and group shots are taken around the venues. Those pictures go up on this site and on NOSTR, where anyone can copy them. Once published, assume it stays published.

  • We do not publish pictures of anyone wearing the X.
  • We avoid close shots of children unless the people with them agree first.
  • We do not tag names to faces, and we do not sell any of it.
  • Anything you post yourself in the chat or the forum is public and is yours to delete.
Changing your mind

Take it down

You can ask at any time, before or long after the event, and you do not have to explain why. We take this seriously and answer quickly.

  • Remove it: the picture or the clip comes down from the site.
  • Blur me: your face is blurred and the picture stays.
  • All of me: we go through the set and remove everything you are in.

Write to the organizers from your messages, which is encrypted, or grab any of us at the event. If a picture is already spread elsewhere we cannot pull it back from other people's copies, but we take down ours and say so.

The short version

Wear the X and you are not in the pictures. Changed your mind later? Message the organizers and it is dealt with.
