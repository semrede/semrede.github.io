Schedule

Five days at Eva Farm with no fixed programme, and one Saturday in the city with times.

Monday to Friday / October 26 to 30

The week at Eva Farm

Nobody hands you a programme. People bring what they want to do and agree it beforehand in the forum or the chat.

What people are putting together

  • Mesh networks tested outdoors, over distance and across hills
  • Taking new tech apart and working out what it does
  • Walks in the forest
  • Swimming in the river
  • Whatever you bring: a workshop, a radio, a problem you want help with

Two things are fixed

Wednesday
afternoon
Tour of old Coimbra

A walk through the old centre of Coimbra. Back at the farm in the evening.

Friday
night
Night out

Out of the farm and into town for the evening.

Propose something, or ask what is already planned, in the Showcase and the rest of the forum.

Saturday / October 31

The open day at Edifício Embaixada

One day in the centre of Coimbra, open to everyone, with a timetable.

10:00
Doors open

Come in, set up, find people. If you are arriving early, the centre is full of places to eat: take your lunch there and be back for two.

14:00
It starts, with music

Music starts. Presentations of the main projects inside the building, project booths outside.

17:00
The grill

Food off the grill. Booths and music carry on.

Want a booth or a slot to present? Put your project in the Showcase.

Times may shift

This page is kept up to date as things firm up. Changes are announced in News in the forum, so watch there if you want to be sure.
